The problem

Surveyors do not scale.
Independent evidence does.

Every insurer already has surveyors and a fraud unit. What no one has is an independent second look at every claim within minutes of it arriving, one that can still be explained line by line months later.

01

Inflated repair estimates become overpayments

When nothing prices the parts independently, an inflated estimate is settled at face value. The overpayment never appears as a loss. It appears as a settled claim.

02

Fraud is visible in the portfolio, not in the queue

Repeat garages, recycled playbooks and staged collisions show up clearly in claim history, but not in front of the adjudicator making today's decision.

03

The regulatory clock does not flex with volume

Surveyor appointment, report and settlement deadlines stay the same through festival spikes and monsoon surges, whatever the queue looks like.

04

A figure you cannot explain cannot settle a claim

Any assessment has to survive an Ombudsman query, an internal audit and the claimant's lawyer asking exactly how the number was reached.

All four are one gap

Evidence arrives faster than anyone can independently check it. ClaimIntel closes that gap on every claim that comes in, not only the ones somebody had time to question.

The shift

From manual review of every claim to evidence-led triage on every claim.

ClaimIntel does not replace investigation. It gives every incoming claim a first, evidence-led layer of assessment, so investigators reach the claims that need them sooner, with the groundwork already done.

Assess

Priced against the market

AI informs. Rules decide.

The assessment is one input. Versioned rules govern the money, and anything that fails to complete holds the claim for review rather than passing it.

Ineligible claims cost nothing

A deterministic eligibility check runs before any AI processing, so a claim outside its policy period or scope of cover is closed without model spend.

What ClaimIntel brings together

Damage assessment

Reads damage from the photographs and reconciles it against the garage estimate: every claimed part confirmed, contradicted or flagged as undeclared, with the evidence marked on the image.

Fraud intelligence

Runs 32 deterministic checks across claim evidence, pricing, documents, telematics and claim history, and scores the claim in code, so the same evidence always scores the same way.

Independent pricing

Benchmarks parts against independent market and OEM sources rather than the estimate being audited, and gives no verdict at all when too few parts can be priced.

Auditable settlement

Applies versioned rules for depreciation, deductibles, total-loss tests and settlement calculations. A regulatory change updates the ruleset, not the code.
